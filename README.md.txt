# Online Food Delivery Platform

## 📌 Project Overview
The **Online Food Delivery Platform** is a web and mobile-based system designed to digitize and streamline the food ordering process.  
It connects **customers**, **restaurants**, **delivery partners**, and **administrators** through a centralized platform, replacing inefficient phone-based ordering with an automated, scalable, and transparent solution.

This repository contains **business, functional, system, and testing documentation** that defines the complete lifecycle of the platform—from problem analysis to user acceptance testing.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Increase online food orders by **40% within 12 months**
- Reduce manual order errors by **60%**
- Achieve customer satisfaction rating of **4.5+**
- Enable scalable operations during peak demand

## ❗ Problem Statement
Traditional phone-based food ordering leads to:
- High order inaccuracies  
- No real-time order tracking  
- Cash-only payments  
- Poor scalability and customer experience  

The platform addresses these challenges by introducing **digital ordering, online payments, and real-time tracking**.

(Reference: Business Problem Document) :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

## 🔍 Solution Summary
The proposed solution enables:
- Customers to browse restaurants and place orders online
- Secure online and cash-on-delivery payments
- Real-time order tracking
- Restaurants to manage orders via a dashboard
- Admins to manage users, restaurants, and reports
