# 🍔 Online Food Delivery Platform – Requirements & Analysis Repository

## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains **end-to-end business and system analysis documentation** for an **Online Food Delivery Platform**.  
The project demonstrates a complete requirements lifecycle—from vision and business needs to functional, system, and testing artifacts.

The platform enables customers to order food online, restaurants to manage orders digitally, delivery partners to fulfill deliveries efficiently, and administrators to monitor operations through analytics and dashboards.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Digitize the complete food ordering and delivery workflow
- Improve customer experience with real-time tracking
- Enable secure and flexible payment options
- Automate delivery partner assignment
- Support data-driven decision-making through reporting and analytics

---

## 🧩 Stakeholders
- **Customers** – Place and track food orders
- **Restaurant Owners** – Manage menus and orders
- **Delivery Partners** – Accept and deliver orders
- **Platform Admins** – Monitor users, orders, and performance
- **Business Owners** – Drive growth and operational efficiency

📘 Document Descriptions
1️⃣ Project Vision Document

Defines the overall vision, goals, scope, stakeholders, success criteria, assumptions, and constraints of the platform.

2️⃣ Business Requirement Document (BRD)

Captures high-level business needs, objectives, scope (in-scope & out-of-scope), stakeholders, constraints, risks, and success metrics.

3️⃣ AS-IS & TO-BE Process Flow

AS-IS: Manual and semi-digital food ordering process

TO-BE: Fully automated, scalable, and transparent workflow
Includes UML activity diagrams, pain points, and business benefits.

4️⃣ Use Case Document

Describes system behavior using:

Actors

Use case diagrams

Detailed use case specifications

Business rules

Exception handling

Sequence diagrams

5️⃣ User Stories

User-centric requirements written from the perspective of:

Customers

Restaurant Owners

Delivery Partners

Admins
Includes priorities, epics, and acceptance criteria.

6️⃣ Functional Requirement Specification (FRS)

Translates business needs into clear, testable functional requirements, organized by modules such as:

User Management

Ordering System

Payments

Delivery Management

Admin & Reporting

7️⃣ System Requirement Specification (SRS)

Defines technical and system-level requirements, including:

Architecture overview

Functional system requirements

Non-functional requirements (performance, security, scalability)

External integrations

Constraints and dependencies

8️⃣ Requirement Management Plan

Explains how requirements are:

Elicited

Prioritized (MoSCoW)

Tracked

Versioned

Approved
Includes roles, governance, tools, and change management workflow.

9️⃣ Requirement Traceability Matrix (RTM)

Ensures 100% requirement coverage by mapping:

Business Requirements → Functional Requirements

Functional Requirements → System Requirements

System Requirements → Test Cases

🔟 Test Case Document

Provides detailed test scenarios and test cases to validate:

Functional correctness

Integration flows

Regression stability

User acceptance readiness
